# Screenshot gallery

The gallery shows native simulator and emulator captures with synthetic data.
Capture dates, dimensions and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in
[the manifest](gallery/manifest.json). The images document the interface; they
do not establish physical-device, Bluetooth or background acceptance.

The maintainers capture and validate images in the private development repository
and export reviewed public files here. Original resolution is preserved.

To report an outdated screenshot or a gallery problem, please
[open a bug report](https://github.com/gatasConspicuity/gatasConnectApp/issues/new/choose).
For questions, use [Discussions](https://github.com/gatasConspicuity/gatasConnectApp/discussions)
or [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/J6mXTcWndS).
